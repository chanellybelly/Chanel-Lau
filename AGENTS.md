# AGENTS.md - AI conventions for Chanel Lau's portfolio

## About me
I am Chanel Lau, a senior at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaii at Manoa, double majoring in Accounting and MIS (expected May 2027). I am working toward a career in IT audit, or another role that uses both majors. I have audit internship experience and work in Excel and Caseware Cloud.

## How I want things explained
- Plain English, one step at a time, with the exact buttons or commands to use
- Define technical terms the first time they appear
- Use worked numeric examples for accounting and finance concepts

## What AI may and may not do
- AI may build setup and mechanical files, explain concepts, check my work, and critique drafts I have written.
- AI may not write my briefs, analyses, memos, or reflections, including first drafts. I write those myself.
- For my bio and resume, AI may draft or review, and I add a disclosure line naming the tool.

## Never paste into an AI
- Client information from my audit work: client names, workpapers, account balances, bank statements, and any Caseware Cloud engagement data
- Financial records I handle for student organizations: budgets, reimbursement requests, and bank details (Inter-Business Council, Beta Alpha Psi)
- Member and employer contact information from my organizations
- Student or faculty records from my office assistant work (these are education records)
- Personal identifiers and secrets: SSN, student ID number, bank or card numbers, passwords, API keys
- Anything covered by a confidentiality agreement

## Naming
- The repository is named for the person (`firstname-lastname`), not for a course.
- Files are named for the engagement, not the week. `perfect-competition-brief.md` makes sense to a stranger in three years; `week1.md` does not.
- Slugs are lowercase and hyphen-separated, three to six words, no spaces or underscores.
- Dated documents lead with the ISO date: `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`.

## Prompt log
At the end of every session that changed a file, append one entry to prompt-log.md: the date, what I asked, what you produced, what was wrong and how it was caught. Never backfill earlier sessions and never edit a past entry.
